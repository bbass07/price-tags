The reliable way

This works today and needs nothing from Bluefy.

  1. Open the Shortcuts app → + → Add Action.
  2. Search Open App, pick it, then choose Bluefy.
  3. Tap the shortcut's name at the top → Add to Home Screen.
  4. Name it Price Tags, tap the icon square → Choose Photo and pick the icon below (long-press it first and save it to Photos).

Bluefy reopens on the page you left, so with fullscreen mode on you land straight in the app. There is a brief flash from Shortcuts on the way through — that is the one rough edge.

Test: can Bluefy be opened by link?

Tap each of these in Safari and let it open Bluefy. Once bluefy:// is registered, iOS offers to open the app for any link starting that way, so "it opened Bluefy" proves nothing by itself. What counts is where Bluefy lands: each link points at a page that shows its own number. Only a scheme that puts a number on screen actually works. If Bluefy just sits on whatever page it had open before, that scheme opened the app and threw the address away.

Between each test, tap through to some other page in Bluefy first — otherwise a page left over from the previous attempt can look like a success.

Do not use Safari's "Add to Home Screen"

It makes a convincing looking app, but it runs on Safari's engine, which has no Bluetooth at all. It will never find the printer.
